# Complytics Icon Generation Prompt (Icon Only, No Text)

## Icon Prompt for Gemini Nano / AI Image Generator:

Create a modern, professional icon for **Complytics** - an AI-powered compliance management platform. This is an **icon-only design** (no text, no wordmark).

**Icon Design Requirements:**

**Core Visual Concept:**
- A stylized letter "C" integrated with compliance/legal symbolism
- OR a shield/badge design with "C" as the central element
- OR scales of justice subtly combined with a "C" shape
- OR a document/checkmark merged with "C" letterform
- The icon should be instantly recognizable and memorable

**Style & Aesthetic:**
- Modern, minimalistic, and professional
- Clean geometric shapes
- Bold and impactful design
- Works well as a standalone symbol
- Suitable for app icons, favicons, and branding

**Visual Elements to Include:**
- Primary: Stylized "C" letterform (can be uppercase or lowercase)
- Secondary elements (choose 1-2, keep subtle):
  - Shield outline or badge shape
  - Scales of justice (very minimal, geometric)
  - Checkmark or verification symbol
  - Document/file icon
  - Security lock or padlock
  - Neural network lines (representing AI - very subtle)
- Keep it simple - maximum 2-3 visual elements total

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep blue (#1e3a8a or #2563eb) - represents trust and security
- Secondary: Teal/Cyan (#06b6d4 or #0891b2) - represents innovation
- Accent: Darker shades (#000000 or #0f172a) for depth and contrast
- Background: Solid color - use deep blue (#1e3a8a) or gradient from blue to teal
- White/light colors for icon elements if needed for contrast
- **NO transparent background** - use solid color background

**Design Specifications:**
- Square format (1:1 aspect ratio)
- High resolution: 1024x1024px minimum
- Icon should be centered and well-balanced
- Works well at small sizes (32x32px for favicon)
- Scalable design (can be vector-based)
- Clear and readable even when scaled down

**What the Icon Should Represent:**
- Compliance and regulatory adherence
- Security and protection
- AI-powered automation
- Trust and reliability
- Legal/regulatory frameworks
- Professional business tool

**Design Concepts:**

**Option 1 - Shield with C:**
- A modern shield/badge shape with a bold "C" inside
- Shield represents security and protection
- Clean, geometric lines
- Blue gradient background

**Option 2 - Scales with C:**
- Minimal geometric scales of justice with "C" integrated
- Represents legal compliance and balance
- Very simplified, modern interpretation
- Deep blue background

**Option 3 - Document Checkmark C:**
- A document/file icon with checkmark, forming a "C" shape
- Represents compliance verification
- Clean, minimal design
- Teal and blue color scheme

**Option 4 - Badge Seal C:**
- A circular badge or seal with "C" as centerpiece
- Represents certification and compliance
- Professional, authoritative look
- Gradient blue background

**What to Avoid:**
- Text or wordmark ("Complytics" text)
- Transparent background (use solid color)
- Purple/violet shades (use darker blues/blacks)
- Overly complex designs (keep it simple)
- Too many elements (maximum 2-3 visual components)
- Unprofessional or playful styles
- Hard-to-read details at small sizes

**Technical Requirements:**
- Format: PNG or JPG (with solid background)
- Resolution: 1024x1024px or higher
- Square aspect ratio (1:1)
- Background: Solid color (deep blue #1e3a8a or gradient blue-to-teal)
- Icon should fill approximately 70-80% of the canvas (with padding around edges)

**Final Output:**
A clean, professional icon that represents compliance management, security, and AI automation. The icon should be memorable, scalable, and work perfectly as an app icon or favicon. Use a solid color background (no transparency) and ensure the design is bold enough to be recognizable at small sizes.

# Complytics Logo Generation Prompt for Gemini Nano / AI Image Generator

## Logo Prompt:

Create a modern, professional logo for **Complytics** - an AI-powered compliance management platform that helps companies manage multiple compliance frameworks (GDPR, ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, WCAG, etc.).

**Logo Design Requirements:**

**Style & Aesthetic:**
- Modern, minimalistic, and professional design
- Clean and uncluttered appearance
- Suitable for both digital and print use
- Trustworthy and authoritative feel
- Tech-forward but approachable

**Visual Elements:**
- Incorporate a stylized "C" letterform as the primary element (can be standalone or combined with icon)
- Include subtle legal/compliance symbolism: scales of justice, shield, checkmark, or document elements (very minimal, not overwhelming)
- Optional: Subtle AI/tech elements like neural network lines or data flow patterns (very faint, barely visible)
- Geometric shapes preferred over organic forms
- Can be icon-only, text-only, or combination mark

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep blue (#1e3a8a or #2563eb) - represents trust and security
- Secondary: Teal/Cyan (#06b6d4 or #0891b2) - represents innovation and technology
- Accent: Darker shades close to black (#000000 or #0f172a) for depth
- White/light colors for contrast
- Gradient option: Blue to teal gradient (subtle, professional)
- Avoid purple shades - use darker tones instead

**Typography (if including text):**
- Modern sans-serif font (similar to Montserrat or DM Sans)
- Bold, clean letterforms
- "Complytics" wordmark should be readable at small sizes
- Can be uppercase, title case, or mixed case

**Logo Variations Needed:**
1. **Primary Logo**: Full logo with icon + "Complytics" text (horizontal layout)
2. **Icon Only**: Just the icon/symbol (square format, works as favicon/app icon)
3. **Wordmark Only**: Just "Complytics" text (for use when icon is separate)

**Technical Specifications:**
- High resolution (at least 1024x1024px for icon, scalable vector preferred)
- Transparent background (PNG with alpha channel)
- Works well at small sizes (favicon: 32x32px)
- Scalable to large sizes (banner: 2000px+ width)
- Format: PNG (with transparency) or SVG

**Design Concepts to Consider:**
- A stylized "C" that incorporates a shield or checkmark
- Scales of justice integrated subtly into the letterform
- A shield with a "C" inside
- A document/checkmark combined with "C"
- Geometric "C" with subtle compliance framework indicators (small dots or lines)
- Modern badge/seal design with "C" as centerpiece

**What to Avoid:**
- Overly complex designs
- Too many colors (stick to 2-3 colors max)
- Purple/violet shades (use darker blues/blacks instead)
- Cluttered or busy elements
- Unprofessional or playful fonts
- Hard-to-read text at small sizes

**Brand Personality:**
- Professional and trustworthy
- Innovative and tech-forward
- Secure and reliable
- Efficient and automated
- Compliance-focused and regulatory-aware

**Final Output:**
Generate a logo that represents compliance management, security, and AI-powered automation in a clean, modern, and professional manner. The logo should work well on both light and dark backgrounds and be instantly recognizable as a compliance/security platform.
